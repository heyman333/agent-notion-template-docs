Background

New sign-ups dropped 18% quarter-over-quarter in Q4. January brings a recurring surge of resolution-driven demand (fitness, savings, learning), so this proposal targets sign-up recovery. Scope: January only, domestic users, in-app promotion and social ads.

Why the challenge format

  1. The reward lands at week three, so it aims straight at the week-2 cliff.
  2. It pairs with invites — completion rates rise when someone starts alongside you.
  3. It moves budget out of paid ads in the month ads cost the most.
This is not a campaign to add sign-ups — it is a campaign to get people past week two.
